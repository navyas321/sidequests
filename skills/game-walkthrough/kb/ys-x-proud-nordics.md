# Ys X: Proud Nordics

- **Edition facts (disambiguation-critical):** `Ys X: Proud Nordics` (2026,
  Switch 2/PS5/PC; JP Jul 2025) is the enhanced edition of `Ys X: Nordics`
  (2024). **Öland Island exists ONLY in Proud Nordics** (unlocks Ch. 5) —
  any base-Nordics guide is the wrong source for Öland content. `EX:`-prefixed
  quests = Proud-exclusive storyline running parallel to the main story
  (chapters gate island access). New Proud systems: Mana Hold (carry/throw),
  Mana-ability upgrades (Sparkling Whitesand), Elding Fragments / mutants,
  skill mastery to 1000%, Bergen Arena coliseum, Mana Ride races vs Canute,
  Muspelheim post-game.
- **Öland zones:** Mainland → East End (dungeon: *Deadman's Blindroad*;
  neoseeker calls it "Blindman's Darkroad") → Midland (cave → Meldal
  Mountain → *Ragnar's Tower*) → Forbidden Land. Circular structure on the
  East End map = training-ground coliseum.
- **Search noise trap:** "Öland investigation" floods with real-world Öland,
  Sweden (ring-fort archaeology, Sandby Borg massacre docs, the Dennis Oland
  murder trial). Filter to game sources.
- **Guide landscape (2026-08):** neoseeker has the most complete Proud
  walkthrough but was still WIP for later EX quests; ludo.guide (56-section
  PS5 guide) good for sea chart/lists; raiderking for Öland buried-treasure
  maps; gamefaqs `ps5/527180` = Proud SKU (390326 = base). Dedicated
  no-commentary quest videos exist per EX quest, titled with exact quest names.

## EX: Öland Island Investigation (I)
- verified: 2026-08-05 | source: neoseeker Ch.5 Öland page
- start: after EX: Suspicious Ship (Hugill's letters), sail to the blue
  undiscovered point north-center of Carnac Waters → event → quest added.
- steps: Mainland first section (Mana Sense panel → switch → mana stream →
  Mana Board across) → coliseum mini-boss gauntlet → Obsidian Key chest →
  blue "?" tower → Deadman's Blindroad (Mana Burst lamp puzzles, Small Steel
  Key red chest, three-lantern dark room) → Hewnstone → boss (Lv 36, ~93k HP)
  → examine Rune Stone → reward Abiding Love + Almighty Star.
- gates: one Red Crest early = either coliseum storage (Luck Elixir) or the
  Blindroad red door (Sin Star+) — can't do both on first pass.

## EX: Öland Island Investigation II
- verified: 2026-08-05 (frame-read from video, user-confirmed route) |
  source: youtube c2D6D-T31Ao "Ys X: Proud Nordics EX: Öland Island
  Investigation II" (75 min, no-commentary)
- start: **blue marker at the circular coliseum on Öland – East End**
  (east/southeast rim of the circle on the map). Reaching it triggers the
  quest's opening scenes.
- steps:
  1. Trigger scenes: Canute starts Mana Ride race contest round 2 (@7:00);
     Astrid opens the Coliseum with a new opponent roster (@1:30–3:00 —
     Jahatmander…Volgadyne R Lv 72; 1st-win rewards incl. Elding Fragment
     x100). Coliseum fights are OPTIONAL side content (~Lv 72).
  2. Objective goes live: **"Reach the Forbidden Land's depths"** (banner
     visible @0:40 on the East End map, persists to @65:00).
  3. Ride across the open field to the great stone gate with the blue
     circular seal (@45:00) → through the cave (story scene @35:00).
  4. Enter **Ragnar's Tower**; climb Lower → Middle Level 1 (@55:00 area
     card; Strength Elixir chest @25:00).
  5. At the depths, hold-**Examine** the glowing rune circle (@65:00) →
     Ragnar flashback cutscene (@72:30) → quest complete. Ends with the race
     contest at 1:1; next leg gated on later abilities ("come back once
     you've picked up the skills needed").
- gates: quest expects Ch. 6-era access (main banner concurrently pushes
  "Head to Specular Bay" — main story and this EX quest are parallel,
  separate threads; answer the one the user asks about).
- pitfalls: video captions are battle voice-lines — reconstructing step
  order from them produced a WRONG walkthrough ("summit tower" fabrication);
  frames' HUD banners are the ground truth. East End buried treasures #6/#7
  need the Adol/Karja split ability and Ch. 9 Mana Board upgrade — not
  reachable during this quest.
